\subsection{What the Solver Actually Forms}
\label{subsec:kkt}

Section~\ref{subsec:ocp} states the model in the solver's generic objects
$x, u, \ell, f, c$.  This subsection eliminates them: every quantity below is
written in the variables of \eqref{eq:cp_all} and the multipliers attached to
its constraints.  All expressions are verified against the derivative functions
the solver actually builds.

\subsubsection{Variables carried at stage $t$}
The state is the stored energy, and nothing else:
\begin{equation}
\underbrace{B^t}_{\text{state}}; \qquad
\underbrace{P_{\text{Subs}}^t,\; P_B^t,\; s^t}_{\text{controls}} .
\label{eq:kkt_vars}
\end{equation}
Each stage carries one multiplier per constraint:
\begin{alignat}{3}
&\lambda_{\text{bal}}^t   &\;\;:\;\;& P_{\text{Subs}}^t + P_B^t - p_L^t = 0
\label{eq:kkt_mult_bal}\\
&\lambda_{\text{soc}}^t    && \bigl(B^{t-1} - P_B^t \Delta t\bigr) - \underline B - s^t = 0
\label{eq:kkt_mult_soc}\\
&\mu_{\underline{P_{\mathrm{Subs}}}}^t && P_{\text{Subs}}^t \geq 0
\label{eq:kkt_mult_ps}\\
&\mu_{\underline{P_B}}^t,\; \mu_{\overline{P_B}}^t && \underline{P_B} \leq P_B^t \leq \overline{P_B}
\label{eq:kkt_mult_pb}\\
&\mu_{\underline B}^t,\; \mu_{\overline B}^t && 0 \leq s^t \leq \overline B - \underline B .
\label{eq:kkt_mult_b}
\end{alignat}
The last line is the correspondence that matters: because $s^t$ is defined by
\eqref{eq:kkt_mult_soc} as the slack of $B^{t}$ above $\underline B$, its two box
multipliers \emph{are} the energy-bound multipliers,
$\mu_{\underline B}^t$ on $B^{t} \geq \underline B$ and $\mu_{\overline B}^t$ on
$B^{t} \leq \overline B$.  There is no separate state-constraint dual.
$P_{\text{Subs}}^t$ has no upper bound, so no multiplier is attached to one.

\subsubsection{Stage derivatives}
With the dynamics $B^{t} = B^{t-1} - P_B^t \Delta t$ linear and the constraints
\eqref{eq:kkt_mult_bal}--\eqref{eq:kkt_mult_soc} linear in the controls, every
second derivative of $f$ and of $c^t$ vanishes, as does the cross term
$\partial^2 \ell^t / \partial u\, \partial B$.  What
remains is
\begin{alignat}{2}
&\frac{\partial c}{\partial(P_{\text{Subs}}, P_B, s)}
  &&= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -\Delta t & -1 \end{bmatrix}
\label{eq:kkt_cu} \\
&\frac{\partial c}{\partial B}
  &&= \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
\label{eq:kkt_cx} \\
&\frac{\partial B^{t}}{\partial P_B^t} &&= -\Delta t
\label{eq:kkt_fu} \\
&\frac{\partial^2 \ell}{\partial (P_{\text{Subs}}, P_B, s)^2}
  &&= \operatorname{diag}\bigl(0,\; 2 C_B \Delta t,\; 0\bigr) .
\label{eq:kkt_luu}
\end{alignat}
The terminal stage adds $2w$ to the $B$ curvature, $2w(\Delta t)^2$ to the
$P_B$ curvature, and $-2w\Delta t$ to their cross term.

\subsubsection{The matrix that is factorised}
Write $V_{BB}^{t+1}$ for the curvature of the cost-to-go in the stored energy.
Collecting \eqref{eq:kkt_fu}--\eqref{eq:kkt_luu} with the barrier terms, the
stage Hessian is \emph{diagonal}:
\begin{equation}
\hat{H}^t = \operatorname{diag}\Bigl(
  \underbrace{\tfrac{\mu_{\underline{P_{\mathrm{Subs}}}}^t}{P_{\text{Subs}}^t}}_{P_{\text{Subs}}},\;\;
  \underbrace{2 C_B \Delta t + (\Delta t)^2 V_{BB}^{t+1} + \Sigma_{P_B}^t}_{P_B},\;\;
  \underbrace{\Sigma_{s}^t}_{s}
\Bigr),
\label{eq:kkt_H}
\end{equation}
\begin{align}
\Sigma_{P_B}^t &= \frac{\mu_{\underline{P_B}}^t}{P_B^t - \underline{P_B}}
                + \frac{\mu_{\overline{P_B}}^t}{\overline{P_B} - P_B^t},
\label{eq:kkt_sigma_pb} \\
\Sigma_{s}^t   &= \frac{\mu_{\underline B}^t}{s^t}
                + \frac{\mu_{\overline B}^t}{\overline B - \underline B - s^t} .
\label{eq:kkt_sigma_s}
\end{align}
Three controls and two equalities leave a \emph{one}-dimensional null space, so
after eliminating \eqref{eq:kkt_cu} exactly one direction survives:
\begin{equation}
d = \bigl(\Delta P_{\text{Subs}},\, \Delta P_B,\, \Delta s\bigr)
  = \bigl(-1,\; +1,\; -\Delta t\bigr) ,
\label{eq:kkt_null}
\end{equation}
which is the statement that the only freedom left at a stage is to move power
between the substation and the battery, the energy slack following.  The
quantity the solver factorises is therefore the \emph{scalar}
\begin{equation}
d^\top \hat{H}^t d
 = \frac{\mu_{\underline{P_{\mathrm{Subs}}}}^t}{P_{\text{Subs}}^t}
 + 2 C_B \Delta t + (\Delta t)^2 V_{BB}^{t+1}
 + \Sigma_{P_B}^t + (\Delta t)^2\, \Sigma_{s}^t ,
\label{eq:kkt_reduced}
\end{equation}
and the Cholesky whose failure triggers regularisation is a positivity test on
this one number.  Dropping the energy bound removes $s^t$ and its two
multipliers, leaving $d = (-1, +1)$ and
$d^\top \hat{H}^t d = \mu_{\underline{P_{\mathrm{Subs}}}}^t / P_{\text{Subs}}^t + 2 C_B \Delta t
+ (\Delta t)^2 V_{BB}^{t+1} + \Sigma_{P_B}^t$; the base instance strips the
barrier terms too, leaving $2 C_B \Delta t + (\Delta t)^2 V_{BB}^{t+1}$, which is
where $C_B$ enters as the curvature that keeps the problem solvable.

\subsubsection{Dimension scaling and IEEE123}
In general the stage matrices have dimensions
\begin{equation}
V_{xx}^t\in\mathbb{R}^{n_x\times n_x},\qquad
\hat H^t\in\mathbb{R}^{n_u\times n_u},\qquad
\hat B^t\in\mathbb{R}^{n_u\times n_x}.
\label{eq:kkt_sizes}
\end{equation}
With $n_c$ stage equalities, the null-space basis is
$Z^t\in\mathbb{R}^{n_u\times(n_u-n_c)}$ and the reduced Hessian is
\begin{equation}
(Z^t)^\top\hat H^t Z^t
\in\mathbb{R}^{(n_u-n_c)\times(n_u-n_c)}.
\label{eq:kkt_reduced_size}
\end{equation}
Thus $T$ does not enlarge an individual stage matrix, but it does multiply the
number of derivative evaluations, factorizations, and stored stage-update
objects.

For the direct IEEE123 single-phase transcription (128 buses, 127 branches,
51 batteries, and 51 PV inverters),
\begin{equation}
n_x=51,\qquad n_u=791,\qquad n_c=562,\qquad n_u-n_c=229.
\label{eq:kkt_ieee123_sizes}
\end{equation}
Consequently, each stage carries a $51\times51$ value Hessian and a
$791\times791$ control Hessian.  The implementation performs a dense QR on a
$791\times791$ matrix, an LU factorization of a $562\times562$ equality block,
and a Cholesky factorization of the $229\times229$ reduced Hessian.  The last
matrix is much smaller than $\hat H^t$, but the dense QR and static-array
storage retain the full network-scale burden.  At $T=2$, symbolic model
construction completed, whereas construction of the solver storage did not
complete after several minutes; one instrumented attempt reached
$6.61$~GB private memory before termination.  Hence the present implementation
is horizon-local in matrix dimension, but not network-scalable without sparse
linear algebra and dynamic storage.

\subsubsection{What is passed backward}
Let $V_B^{t}$ denote the marginal value of energy \emph{held at the end of
period $t$}, that is the derivative of the remaining cost-to-go with respect to
$B^{t}$; $V_{BB}^{t}$ is its curvature.  With a one-dimensional state both are
scalars, so the whole backward recursion is scalar arithmetic:
\begin{equation}
V_B^{t-1} = V_B^{t} + \lambda_{\text{soc}}^t
        + \bigl(\beta^\top \hat{Q}_u^t\bigr)_B + \bigl(\omega^\top c^t\bigr)_B ,
\label{eq:kkt_Vx}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
V_{BB}^{t-1} = V_{BB}^{t} + \bigl(\beta^\top \hat{B}^t\bigr)_{BB}
           + \bigl(\omega^\top c_x^t\bigr)_{BB} ,
\label{eq:kkt_Vxx}
\end{equation}
where $\beta$ and $\omega$ are the feedback gains produced by the stage solve.
At a feasible iterate ($c^t = 0$) that has converged ($\hat{Q}_u^t = 0$),
\eqref{eq:kkt_Vx} collapses to
\begin{equation}
V_B^{t-1} - V_B^{t} = \lambda_{\text{soc}}^t ,
\label{eq:kkt_costate}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the marginal value of stored energy is the discrete costate, and the drop
in that value across period $t$ is exactly the multiplier on that period's
energy relation.
Equation~\eqref{eq:kkt_Vxx} has no counterpart in a first-order method: it is the
curvature, and it is the object that a scheme passing only
\eqref{eq:kkt_costate} between stages does not carry.

\subsubsection{What is passed forward}
With step size $\gamma$ and $\delta B = B^t - \bar{B}^t$ the deviation from the
nominal trajectory, every quantity is updated by the same affine rule:
\begin{alignat}{2}
P_{\text{Subs}}^t &= \bar{P}_{\text{Subs}}^t + \gamma\,\alpha_{\text{Subs}}^t
   + \beta_{\text{Subs}}^t\, \delta B , \quad&
P_B^t &= \bar{P}_B^t + \gamma\,\alpha_{B}^t + \beta_{B}^t\, \delta B ,
\label{eq:kkt_fwd_u} \\
\lambda_\bullet^t &= \bar{\lambda}_\bullet^t + \gamma\,\psi_\bullet^t
   + \omega_\bullet^t\, \delta B , &
\mu_\bullet^t &= \bar{\mu}_\bullet^t + \gamma\,\chi_\bullet^t
   + \zeta_\bullet^t\, \delta B ,
\label{eq:kkt_fwd_mult}
\end{alignat}
so the primal variables, the equality multipliers and the bound multipliers are
all propagated together along the same search direction, with a single $\gamma$
accepted by the filter for all of them.
