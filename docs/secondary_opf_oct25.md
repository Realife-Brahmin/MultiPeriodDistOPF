# Secondary-feeder OPF results (25 October 2026)

The interactive viewer retains the five historical T24/78-direction cases and adds
explicitly labeled new exploratory four-source screens. New surfaces are **import-only
predictions**, not fitted or certified feasible regions. Measured points are independently
verified nonlinear OPF endpoints. Sparse axis samples cannot establish volume or face
planarity. Horizon, actual coverage and equal-angle status appear in each new tab.

Numerical evidence and full-day two/three-source IEEE8500 results:
[research archive](https://github.com/Realife-Brahmin/MultiPeriodDistOPF/tree/msopf-oct24-test-system-edition/problems/msopf/results/secondary_8500_oct25).

[Updated manuscript and compiled PDF](https://github.com/Realife-Brahmin/PESGM2027_Multi-Source-Multi-Period-OPF).

New T6 screens use six four-hour periods. Existing profile resampling changes daily PV
energy with T; these are exploratory results, **not** interchangeable with T24 windows.
The archive preserves solver timeouts, failed replay candidates and actual radial
brackets. These omissions are not relabeled physical infeasibility. The matched
primary-proxy cases' negative local margins do not prove globally empty regions.
