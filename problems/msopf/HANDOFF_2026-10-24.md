## Decisions (the user's)

- **Decks with secondaries.** Use the `*xf_1ph` decks: each service transformer is a line to its secondary bus, and
  the loads sit there. The 120/240 V triplex lines are not modelled.
- **Voltage band at customer buses only.** The 0.95-1.05 band applies only at customer (secondary) buses; the
  primary has no band.
  - Solver: `solve_multiperiod(...; band_buses = Set(customer buses))`.
  - Customer buses are the `to` end of each line named `svc_*`.
- **Up to four substations:** two-, three- and four-source cases only.
- **DER rule.** Our own PV and batteries:
  - each on about 10% of primary buses, a third of those buses sharing both;
  - each fleet 40% of total load (a 50% version was reverted).
- **Standing rules.** No new grid assets. PV reactive power is always dispatched (`qfree`). Terminal SOC is hard
  (cyclic).

**P1U ties.** SMART-DS publishes its normally-open ties as disabled switches. `convert_to_1ph.jl` reads them, giving
39 ties, 6 of them between substations (chain hs0-hs2-hs1-hs3). The deck folder has two variant masters:

- `Master_4sub_typeIII.dss`: hs0 on one transformer, its second section fed through the bus tie; the 6
  inter-substation ties closed. This is the usual problem.
- `Master_4sub_typeII.dss`: hs0's two transformers tied through their feeders; hs3 off.

## Results so far

**Smoke test.** Driver `problems/msopf/root_level/test_edition_smoke.jl`; results in
`problems/msopf/results/test_networks/smoke.csv`.

- Setup: equal angles, T = 24, PV reactive power and batteries dispatched, cyclic SOC, band at customer buses.
- All five decks solve, in 16-105 Ipopt iterations and 5-120 s each.
- The OpenDSS replay matches to 1e-4 kW.
- The 8500 needs DER support to keep its customers at 0.95 pu.

**Topology figures.** `problems/msopf/root_level/plot_test_networks.py` writes
`problems/msopf/results/test_networks/*.png` and `gallery.png`.

## How to run

```bash
unset NO_COLOR FORCE_COLOR
julia --startup-file=no --project=problems/msopf problems/msopf/root_level/test_edition_smoke.jl
```

- Run Julia through Bash, from the repo root.
- `problems/msopf/Manifest.toml` is no longer tracked after the restructure. Copy it from
  `problems/mpopf/tadmm/Manifest.toml`, which has the identical Project.
- The SDP or Clarabel scripts run in `problems/msopf/environments/sdp`.
