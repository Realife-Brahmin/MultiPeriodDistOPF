Certified Feasibility Maps

Angle differences between the substations (held over a 24-hour day) at which a feasible dispatch exists, for the paper's three cases. Along each direction from equal angles, a validated AC dispatch (checked in OpenDSS) and a Farkas certificate bracket the edge. Every line is held to its rated current; each certificate also uses the current bounds the network itself implies at that point, and was recomputed in exact rational arithmetic. Drag to rotate or pan, scroll to zoom, click a point for its numbers.
