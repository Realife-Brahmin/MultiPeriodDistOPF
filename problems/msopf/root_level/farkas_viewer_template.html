Certified Feasibility Maps

Which upstream angle differences can the feeder live with? At equal angles (the centre) it works. A direction is one fixed recipe for tilting the angles apart, for example "raise substation 2 only" or "raise 2 and lower 3 equally"; along it we push the tilt further until no valid dispatch exists. Two substations have one angle difference, so just 2 directions (+ and −); three have a plane, sampled every 22.5° (16 directions); four have a 3-D space, sampled by 78 directions spread evenly over a sphere like golf-ball dimples. Each system has four substations, and every pair (6) and triple (4) of them is studied as well. Each direction is bracketed from both sides (Farkas bracketing): green, a validated AC dispatch at 98% of the edge; red, a sparse semidefinite relaxation proving every angle beyond infeasible. The thin gap between them is all that remains unresolved. Batteries idle, at each direction's limiting hour; certificates hold for the model with empirical current limits (the largest currents of the validated dispatches).
