## Conclusions limited to this configuration and horizon

- Removing implied ell>=0 is consistently beneficial across all four
  contexts per system. Added alone it reduces time by18.0%,19.4%,28.2%
  on ieee123/med2522/large10k. With the other two rules already on, its
  marginal savings are28.0%,16.7%,36.3%, respectively.
- Upper-voltage screening alone is NOT an unconditional speedup: it slows
  all three systems versus mask0. With ell already screened it helps
  ieee123 and large10k but slows med2522. Removing more inequalities does
  not guarantee fewer iterations: their barriers alter the iteration path.
- Import-bound screening changes medians by roughly0--2%, with mixed signs;
  do not claim a meaningful robust gain with two repeats.
- All optional rules versus substation-only saves23.8%,2.9%,32.3%.
  med2522's fastest tested combination screens ell but retains upper-voltage
  limits (mask2), rather than screening everything. No defaults changed.
- Iterations mask0 -> mask7:33->24,66->66,30->16. Every mask has repeated
  identical iteration counts except large10k mask6 (16/18); timings have
  noticeable order/node drift, especially large10k. Keep ranges and
  conditional comparisons; do not rank small differences as significant.
