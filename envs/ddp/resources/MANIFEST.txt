GPU-resident-sparse-direct-linear-solvers-for-ACOPF-2023-Sep.pdf | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.14337 | Swirydowicz, Koukpaizan, Ribizel, Goebel, Abhyankar, Anzt, Peles, "GPU-resident sparse direct linear solvers for alternating current optimal power flow analysis", International Journal of Electrical Power and Energy Systems, vol. 155, 109517, 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.ijepes.2023.109517 (open access, CC BY-NC-ND), arXiv 2306.14337. First GPU-native sparse direct solver running on both AMD and NVIDIA GPUs, for the KKT systems of ACOPF interior-point methods; reports linear solves as typically more than half of total compute. NOTE: the file on disk is the published journal version; the url fetches the arXiv preprint, because the ScienceDirect DOI page is not curl-fetchable. Added 2026-09-18 (from Anamika).
Accelerating-OPF-with-GPUs-SIMD-abstraction-of-NL-programs-and-Condensed-Space-IP-method-2024-Nov.pdf | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.16830 | Shin, Anitescu, Pacaud, "Accelerating optimal power flow with GPUs: SIMD abstraction of nonlinear programs and condensed-space interior-point methods", Electric Power Systems Research, vol. 236, 110651, 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.epsr.2024.110651, arXiv 2307.16830. Condenses the indefinite KKT system into a positive-definite one so it can be factorized WITHOUT numerical pivoting, which permits refactorization with a fixed pivot sequence; MadNLP.jl + ExaModels.jl on NVIDIA GPUs, an order of magnitude faster than CPU tools. Directly relevant to FilterDDP's pivoting-driven fill-in and to factor reuse. NOTE: file on disk is the published version; the url fetches the arXiv preprint. Added 2026-09-18 (from Anamika).
Condensed-IP-Methods-for-Scalable-NLP-on-GPUs-2026-Jul.pdf | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.14236 | Pacaud, Shin, Montoison, Schanen, Anitescu, "Condensed Interior-Point Methods for Scalable Nonlinear Programming on GPUs", arXiv 2405.14236 (file on disk is v3, 21 Jul 2026). Error analysis and benchmarks of two condensed-space IPM variants, HyKKT and LiftedKKT: the condensed systems are more ill-conditioned, but their structure offsets the accuracy loss. Fully GPU-resident MadNLP + cuDSS (sparse Cholesky) + ExaModels on pglib-opf and CUTEst. Added 2026-09-18 (from Anamika).
JAX-Based-Batched-ACPF-for-GPU-Acceleration-and-AI-Ecosystem-Integration-2026-May.pdf | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.14103 | Zhou, Cope, Foerster, Morstyn (Oxford), "JAX-Based Batched AC Power Flow for GPU Acceleration and AI Ecosystem Integration", IEEE Power Engineering Letters per the arXiv header, arXiv 2605.14103v1, May 2026. Batched AC power flow in JAX: Newton-Raphson with GMRES for transmission, Z-Bus for three-phase unbalanced distribution; more than 10x faster than pandapower and OpenDSS. Added 2026-09-18 (from Anamika).
